# Hint 1: where to look

No code yet, just where everything is.

- **The rpc.** In [`auction.proto`](../../proto/auction/v1/auction.proto): `rpc PlaceBid(PlaceBidRequest) returns
  (PlaceBidResponse)`. Look at the fields of `PlaceBidRequest`, then at `PlaceBidBody` in
  [`bff/Dtos.cs`](../../bff/Dtos.cs). Notice anything?
- **The client.** It's generated from the proto, and [`bff/Program.cs`](../../bff/Program.cs) registers it for
  dependency injection. Your endpoint doesn't ask for it yet: that's the `TODO`. Every `rpc Foo` gets a `FooAsync`
  method on it.
- **The template.** `GET /auction` in [`bff/AuctionEndpoints.cs`](../../bff/AuctionEndpoints.cs) does five things:
  get the client injected, build a request, call the rpc, turn the response into JSON for the browser, and turn gRPC
  errors into HTTP errors. Yours needs the same five.
