# Hint 1: where to look

No code yet, just where everything is.

- **The rpc.** In [`auction.proto`](../../proto/auction/v1/auction.proto): `rpc PlaceBid(PlaceBidRequest) returns
  (PlaceBidResponse)`. Look at the fields of `PlaceBidRequest`, then at `PlaceBidBody` in
  [`bff/Dtos.cs`](../../bff/Dtos.cs). Notice anything?
- **The client.** It's generated, and it's already injected into your endpoint as `auction`. Every `rpc Foo` gets
  a `FooAsync` method.
- **The template.** `GET /auction`, right above your endpoint, does four things: build a request, call the rpc,
  turn the response into JSON for the browser, and turn gRPC errors into HTTP errors. Yours needs the same four.
